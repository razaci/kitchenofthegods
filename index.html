How to Play

1. Pick ingredients

Open Pantry and tap ingredients. Each item has a quantity. Selected ingredients are moved to the Kitchen Station.

2. Pick the right cooking method

Recipes care about method. Eggs can become scrambled eggs when sautéed, but boiling them produces a different result.

3. Follow recipes—or experiment

Exact or near-exact combinations score higher. Experiments still produce a dish and are rated for flavor balance using ingredient categories.

4. Flavor logic

Salt enhances savory foods, fats add richness, acids brighten dishes, aromatics add depth, and excessive conflicting ingredients lower the score.

5. No fake medical or nutrition claims

This is a cooking game, not health advice. Food safety matters in real kitchens: cook meats and eggs safely, avoid cross-contamination, and follow reliable food-safety guidance.
